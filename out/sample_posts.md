## 1. [SHIP] v3 - Supermarket shelf walk-ranking, one item deliberately left unnamed, winner from the wrong aisle  (score 9/10)

**Hook** visual: Handheld walk down a Tesco aisle, hand grabs a tub off the shelf without stopping | audio: Best high protein snack in Tesco isn't in the protein section. | text: **Best protein snack in Tesco**

**Script**
- Ranking Tesco protein snacks by whether I'd actually eat them twice.
- Bottom of the list, the chalky bar in the gold wrapper. Great macros, tastes like a candle.
- Middle, the protein yoghurts. Boring, does the job, no notes.
- Top three is where it gets annoying.
- Third, tinned tuna. Cheapest protein in the building and nobody wants to hear it.
- Second, the chicken skewers in the chilled bit by the tills.
- First isn't in the protein aisle at all. It's cottage cheese.
- Call it grim in the comments and I'll show you the only way I eat it.

**On-screen text:** last: the gold wrapper one / mid: protein yoghurt / #3 tinned tuna / #2 chicken skewers / #1 not in the protein aisle

**Caption:** Cottage cheese, grim or elite?  
**~32s**

**Share reason:** Sent to whoever does the weekly shop, because it turns into a list they actually use  
**Comment trigger:** The unnamed gold wrapper bar plus a cottage cheese take people cannot leave alone

**Why views:** It reuses the highest performing format on the account with a new shelf, and the hook denies the obvious answer in the first line.

Scores: hook_strength 5, specificity 5, retention 4, share_trigger 5, native 5, honesty 5, reusability 5

Editor fix: Cut "Middle, the protein yoghurts. Boring, does the job, no notes." It is the only line that does not move the viewer forward; reinvest the two seconds in the cottage cheese reveal so the payoff lands harder before the drop-off point.

## 2. [bench] v1 - 3-item ranking with the controversial pick last (worst-to-first, one pick withheld)  (score 8/10)

**Hook** visual: Phone-shot, hand slaps the seat of a seated twist machine, fast whip-pan across the gym floor | audio: Ranking gym machines by how much of your time they're stealing. | text: **Worst gym machines, ranked**

**Script**
- Ranking gym machines by how much of your time they're stealing.
- Number three, the seated twist machine. Your spine isn't a door hinge.
- Number two, the abductor machine. Perfect if the goal is looking busy.
- Number one is the one there's always a queue for.
- The chest press. It's a bench press with training wheels bolted on.
- Same weight on dumbbells and you'll feel where your chest actually stops working.
- I left the treadmill out because that one's on you, not the machine.
- And yes, I still use one of these three every week. Guess which one.

**On-screen text:** #3 seated twist / #2 abductor / #1 always a queue / swap it: dumbbell press / guess which one I still use

**Caption:** Which one am I wrong about?  
**~30s**

**Share reason:** Gym regulars send it to the mate who lives on the machines, as a soft insult with plausible deniability  
**Comment trigger:** A ranking people will defend, plus the withheld admission that one of the three is still in my programme

**Why views:** Ranking format is the account's strongest performer and the hook promises a fight in the first second instead of context.

Scores: hook_strength 4, specificity 4, retention 4, share_trigger 5, native 5, honesty 4, reusability 5

Editor fix: Replace the generic "Worst gym machines, ranked" overlay with the fight-picking claim itself ("the #1 worst one always has a queue") so the withheld reveal is loaded in frame one, not just in the voiceover.

## 3. [bench] v2 - Mistake-callout with instant wrong-rep / right-rep visual proof  (score 8/10)

**Hook** visual: Tight shot on a lat pulldown bar freezing at forehead height mid-rep, then frozen frame | audio: That rep stopped early, so the whole set just did nothing. | text: **you stopped three inches early**

**Script**
- That rep stopped early, so the whole set just did nothing.
- Watch the bar. It never reaches the chest.
- Now the same weight, full range, and watch the face.
- Half the reps, and it feels twice as heavy.
- The mistake isn't the weight you picked, it's where you decided to stop.
- If you have to shorten the rep to move it, it isn't your weight yet.
- Take a plate off, finish the rep, then tell me your back didn't wake up.
- Then go and check if you're doing the exact same thing on rows.

**On-screen text:** wrong / right / same weight / range of motion > numbers / now check your rows

**Caption:** Ego lifting, or nobody ever told you? Be honest.  
**~26s**

**Share reason:** Sent from one training partner to another as a call-out mid-week, and saved by beginners who suspect they do it  
**Comment trigger:** Half the comments will insist partial reps are fine, the other half will tag someone

**Why views:** Mistake-callouts already outperform on this account and the error is visible before a single word lands, so the first frame carries the hook.

Scores: hook_strength 5, specificity 3, retention 4, share_trigger 4, native 4, honesty 3, reusability 5

Editor fix: Soften "the whole set just did nothing" to something you can actually show ("you just skipped the part that works"), and add one concrete number, the weight on the bar, so the wrong-rep/right-rep comparison has a stake instead of a vague claim.

## 4. [bench] v4 - Silent POV text-overlay hot take over trending audio, one plain opinion per beat  (score 6/10)

**Hook** visual: Close, slow shot of hands sliding a plate onto a barbell, gym mirror behind, no face | audio: silent / trending audio | text: **Soreness is not progress**

**Script**
- Soreness is not progress.
- It only means the session was new.
- Progress is the same lift, one more rep, next week.
- If you need pain to feel like it counted, you'll keep starting over.
- The thing that actually changed for me was writing the numbers down.
- Boring weeks are the ones doing the building.
- Now tell me the most overrated feeling in the gym.

**On-screen text:** new ≠ effective / same lift, one more rep / no notes, no progress / boring = working / most overrated gym feeling?

**Caption:** Most overrated feeling in the gym? Go.  
**~22s**

**Share reason:** Sent by lifters to the friend who brags about not being able to walk after leg day  
**Comment trigger:** A hot take stated flatly, and a direct question that invites a ranking in the replies

**Why views:** Silent text hook removes the ad feel entirely and the opinion is a contradiction of common belief in six words, which is read before the thumb moves.

Scores: hook_strength 3, specificity 2, retention 2, share_trigger 3, native 4, honesty 4, reusability 5

Editor fix: The hook has no visible stake, plate loading is the most scrolled-past shot in fitness. Open on something that contradicts the text on screen (limping down stairs, or a notes app screenshot of last week's numbers) and specify a visual change every beat, otherwise the middle is seven text cards and dies at line three.

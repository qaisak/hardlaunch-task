# Cal AI: long overlay

**Format decision:** long_overlay. Cal AI is an app whose pain is entirely emotional and relatable, the 11pm counter scroll through a food database for a curry with no label, and that lands harder as a silent reacting person with one block of text than as a screen recording. It also needs no brand-uploaded footage, so the old way (scales, notes app, googling calories) does all the work and the app arrives as the payoff in the last line. Runner-up hook_demo: The photo scan is genuinely satisfying to watch, but a demo-led opener reads as an ad within a second and the brand has not supplied demo footage for a first awareness post.

## The post

> watched a lad ask chatgpt how many calories in his mum's lasagne. described the whole dish. portion size. type of mince. no scales. no cal ai. no label. then argued with it when it said 900. sat there negotiating with a robot over lasagne. genuinely unwell

**Base video:** Creator on a sofa with a plate on their lap, mid bite, looking off camera like they are watching someone across the room, then straight down the lens with raised eyebrows.  
**Audio:** original audio, TV murmuring in the background  
**Caption:** he got it down to 740 in the end  
**Why it is this brand:** Talking a chatbot into a lower number for a homemade dish is the guesswork loop Cal AI's meal scan is built to end.

Score 9/10. Editor: tighten the opening to 'watched a lad ask chatgpt how many calories in his mum's lasagne' and drop 'type of mince' so the escalation lands faster.

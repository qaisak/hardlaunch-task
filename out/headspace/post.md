# Headspace: long overlay

**Format decision:** long_overlay. Headspace is an app with no physical demo, and its real hook is the 1am bedroom scene where someone lies awake after doomscrolling, melatonin gummies and a YouTube sleep playlist, which a silent reacting person plus one block of on-screen text nails better than any screen recording. The old way here is universally relatable, so the text block carries the whole emotional load and gets shared to the friend who is also awake at 1am. Runner-up slideshow: A meditation app has little visual before/after to show in images, so slides would end up as quote cards that stall completion.

## The post

> me and a four year old staring each other down at 8:40 like it is the old west. third attempt tonight. no sleepcast. no white noise machine. no headspace. no lavender anything. just two people fully wired and neither of them blinking. Two maniacs in this house. one of them is four

**Base video:** Parent sitting on the floor outside a kid's bedroom door in the hallway, holding a stuffed animal, looking dead into the camera with zero expression.  
**Audio:** soft music box or nursery melody, slightly slowed  
**Caption:** who is winning  
**Why it is this brand:** Sleepcasts and the third bedtime attempt are Headspace's parent use case, and lavender spray is the crutch every tired parent actually owns.

What changed: Moved the 8:40 staredown image into the opening words and pushed the third-attempt line back, keeping the same list, escalation and verdict.

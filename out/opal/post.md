# Opal: long overlay

**Format decision:** long_overlay. Opal is an app with no physical demo moment, and its whole pull is the relatable 'old way' pile: deleting Instagram then redownloading it, airplane mode, phone in another room. A silent reacting person at 11:40pm in bed with one block of text about losing the night to 'one more video' lets the pain land in 1.5 seconds and the product arrive as the discovery, not the announcement. Runner-up slideshow: A carousel of old-way screenshots could work, but Opal has no strong visual before/after to swipe through and slides lose the face-plus-audio hold that drives first-post reach.

# Blank Street: long overlay

**Format decision:** long_overlay. Blank Street's pull is the relatable morning ritual, the clumpy fork-whisked matcha at home and the queue you don't have time for, which lands hardest as a silent reacting face plus one block of text about the old way. It also avoids needing brand-supplied demo footage, and app ordering is a behaviour change more than a thing to watch working. Runner-up hook_demo: A drink being handed over is a weak demo and it needs footage the brand would have to shoot first.

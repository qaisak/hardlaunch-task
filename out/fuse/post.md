# Fuse: long overlay

**Format decision:** long_overlay. Fuse is an app-led energy service with a painfully relatable old way: squinting at the in-home display, 18 minutes of hold music just to ask why the direct debit jumped. A silent reacting person over one block of text lets that whole story land in 20 seconds without needing footage of a product that is really just a bill, and the app benefit gets discovered at the end rather than announced. Runner-up hook_demo: It needs real demo footage and the interesting bit, bills updating every 30 minutes, is a screen recording that reads as an ad before the hook has earned attention.

**Base video:** Creator stands in a dim kitchen holding a mug, slowly looks off camera then back at the lens with a flat stare. No speaking, tiny head shake at the end.  
**Audio:** original audio, room tone  
**Caption:** how much do you reckon that load of towels cost him
**Why it is this brand:** The in-home display and pricing a tumble dryer load is the exact UK smart meter panic Fuse's 30 minute forecasts exist for.

Draft scored 7/10. Editor fix applied: 'no fuse' sits oddly between 'no app' and 'no forecast' since app and forecast are the same idea; swap one for a concrete object like 'no spreadsheet' and tighten the verdict, 'tea leaves in a power station' is one word too clever to be deadpan.  
What changed: Swapped the overlapping 'no forecast' for the concrete 'no spreadsheet' and cut the over-clever tea leaves line to a blunter verdict.

## Runners-up

- (7/10) i moved flat last year and spent moving day kneeling in a cupboard photographing a meter. no idea who supplied the place. no account. no fuse. just me, a torch on my phone and a camera roll of eleven blurry meter photos i never looked at again. Absolute clown behaviour from me
- (7/10) girl at the bus stop reading the price cap headline out loud to her boyfriend. no comparison site open. no fixed tariff. no fuse. still on the standard variable she got put on in 2021. reckons she'll switch when the news says it's a good time. The news is not going to tell her
- (7/10) my landlord manages four flats off one spreadsheet of meter readings. colour coded. one tab per property. no dashboard. no single account. no fuse. he types the numbers in on a sunday night with the football on mute. says it keeps him sharp. Sir that is a hobby not a system
- (7/10) watched my neighbour plug the ev in at half four in the afternoon. peak rate. no off peak window set. no charger linked. no fuse. he says he charges when he gets home because that's when he gets home. then complains about the bill in the driveway every month. Respect the consistency nothing else
- (5/10) my mate has been on hold in the car park outside work for eighteen minutes. same four bars of hold music on loop. no live chat. no 24 hour line. no fuse. all he wants to know is why the direct debit went up by forty quid. he's turned the engine back on to keep warm. Genuinely heroic and genuinely mental

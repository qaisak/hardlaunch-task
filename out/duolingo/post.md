# Duolingo: long overlay

**Format decision:** long_overlay. Duolingo is an app with no physical demo and a very relatable old way: five years of school French and still freezing at a Paris bakery counter, which lands hardest as a silent reaction to a block of text. The meme-literate, self-aware tone is exactly what this format rewards, and the streak anxiety angle is a built-in comment trigger without needing any footage from the brand. Runner-up slideshow: A language app has nothing visual to carousel through, so slides would just be text on images doing the same job with weaker hold in the first 1.5 seconds.

## The post

> just seen a guy order in french at a bakery counter. no google translate. no phrasebook. no duolingo. no notes app vocab list. two years of school textbooks and pure nerve against an entire country. the queue went silent. Absolute lunatic behaviour

**Base video:** Creator sitting outside a cafe holding a coffee, looking off to the side at someone out of frame, then a slow blinking double take straight down the lens. No talking.  
**Audio:** original audio, room tone with faint street noise  
**Caption:** would you have risked it or pointed at the croissant  
**Why it is this brand:** The bakery-counter freeze after years of school French is the exact language-learner moment Duolingo users joke about.

## Runners-up

- (8/10) guy on the 8am bus had a paper vocab notebook out. ruled columns. verbs down the left. no anki. no duolingo. no headphones. no audio course. just him and a pen against an entire tense system. missed his stop writing out the past perfect. did not even look annoyed. Some kind of monk
- (7/10) i spent eight months learning my partner's family language from her grandmother at sunday dinner. no flashcards. no duolingo. no youtube grammar videos. no google translate under the table. just me nodding at questions i could not properly hear. found out last week i have been saying yes to more soup this entire time. Nobody tell her
- (7/10) the guy i live with paid for an evening class up front. tuesday nights. a printed workbook. no app. no flashcards. no duolingo on the phone he stares at for nine hours a day. forty minutes each way to conjugate verbs in a school hall with nine strangers and a whiteboard. Committed. Deranged. Both
- (7/10) my sister deleted a 400 day streak on purpose. no duolingo. no notes app vocab list. no flashcards. said she was going to just speak to people instead. walked into a language exchange night with eleven words and one insult she learned off a film. came back with three new friends and zero dignity. She is winning and it is annoying
- (7/10) girl at the airport revising greek from a phrasebook printed in 2009 she found in a second hand shop. no duolingo. no offline dictionary. no google translate downloaded. plane lands in three hours and she is deep in the chapter about posting a parcel. Nobody is going to the post office

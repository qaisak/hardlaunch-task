# Duolingo: long overlay

**Format decision:** long_overlay. Duolingo is an app with no physical thing to watch working, but a very strong relatable 'old way': the January flashcards, the unopened grammar textbook, the evening class you paid for and quit after week two. A silent reacting face over one block of text lets the joke about abandoning every other method land in under two seconds, which is where the hook hold rate lives. Runner-up slideshow: Slideshows suit visual or before/after products, and screenshots of lessons read as an ad the moment they appear, killing retention on a first awareness post.

## The post

> just seen a lad on the 43 bus trying to learn german off a textbook. paper one. no app. no duolingo. no flashcards. no google translate. just him and a grammar table from 1998 at 8am on a wet tuesday. bookmark was a receipt. respect honestly.

**Base video:** Creator sitting on the top deck of a bus, hood up, phone propped at chest height, looking straight down the lens then glancing sideways at something off camera. Tiny eyebrow raise, no speaking.  
**Audio:** original audio, room tone with bus engine  
**Caption:** the receipt bookmark is what got me  
**Why it is this brand:** Learning German off a paper grammar table on a commute is exactly the ten-spare-minutes moment Duolingo owns.

Score 8/10. Editor: cut "no app." since it duplicates the duolingo item, and swap "respect honestly" for a fresher verdict that points at the receipt bookmark so the caption and text stop repeating each other.

## Runners-up

- (8/10) just seen my brother at his desk at 11pm getting ready to learn french. unopened grammar textbook. three paper flashcards he wrote in january. anki downloaded never opened. no duolingo. just him and a highlighter staring at page one like it owes him money. genuinely unwell behaviour.
- (7/10) i went to madrid for nine days. no duolingo. no phrasebook. no notes app vocab list. just me and three words i half remembered from year 8. ordered every single meal in english while apologising in english about it. came home saying i'd pick it up next time. couldn't be me. except it was me.
- (7/10) my flatmate signed up for an evening class at the college. forty minutes each way in the rain. no app on the bus there. no duolingo. no flashcards. no youtube videos. just her a whiteboard and six other adults conjugating verbs at half seven on a wednesday. she's built different and wrong.
- (7/10) girl in the departure lounge at stansted buying a paper phrasebook for a week in italy. no app. no duolingo. no youtube grammar. no notes app list. just her and eighty laminated pages she's going to read once on the plane and never open again. hope she makes it honestly.

# Jenni: long overlay

**Format decision:** long_overlay. Jenni is a SaaS workspace with a painfully relatable old way: 40 PDF tabs at 1am and hand-reformatting APA to Harvard the night before submission, which a silent reacting academic plus one text block lands instantly without needing demo footage. The product benefit (every claim traced to a real source) is discovered as the punchline of the text, not announced, which keeps it native to academic TikTok. Runner-up hook_demo: It needs real screen-capture demo footage of the citation and Reviews flow that the brand has not supplied, and a first awareness post should sell the pain, not the interface.

# Allbirds: long overlay

**Format decision:** long_overlay. Allbirds' strongest awareness angle is the relatable old way, boiling feet on an August tube and three pairs of shoes crammed into a carry on, which lands faster as silent reacting person plus one text block than as footage of a fairly normal-looking shoe. The shoe itself doesn't demo dramatically, so the pain story carries the views and the product is discovered at the end rather than announced. Runner-up hook_demo: A wool trainer has no visible 'working' moment on camera, so a demo stitch would just be a shoe sitting there and would need footage the brand has to shoot first.

## The post

> just seen a guy on the victoria line in august. synthetic trainers. gym socks. shoe spray sat in his tote bag like a first aid kit. no insoles. no allbirds. just him and four stops of pure heat and his own choices. Respect honestly.

**Base video:** Creator sat on a tube seat or standing by the doors, holding the pole, glancing off camera then slowly back to lens with a tiny eyebrow raise. Sweaty fringe, normal commute clothes, no talking.  
**Audio:** original audio, room tone with tube rumble  
**Caption:** august on the underground is a personality test  
**Why it is this brand:** The shoe spray plus synthetic trainers plus an August tube carriage is the exact sweaty-commuter pain Allbirds' wool and tree fibre shoes exist against.

Score 8/10. Editor: the no-list is only two items and one of them is the brand. add a real crutch ("no insoles. no shoe spray. no allbirds.") and swap "Respect honestly" for a verdict people can argue with.

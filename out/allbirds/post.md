# Allbirds: long overlay

**Format decision:** long_overlay. Allbirds' strongest hook isn't the shoe on screen, it's the shared misery of blister plasters, gel insoles and unlacing stiff trainers at 6am airport security, and a silent reacting face plus one text block lets that whole 'old way' story land in 1.5 seconds without sounding like a shoe ad. The comfort payoff is felt, not seen, so a demo clip has nothing visual to prove, while relatable text does the persuading and drives the shares and 'this is me' comments that awareness posts live on. Runner-up hook_demo: Comfort, wool and tree fibre can't be demonstrated on camera the way a gadget can, so the demo footage would just be shoes rotating while the real story sits in the voiceover.

## The post

> day three of the trip. she's on a curb peeling a sock off a blistered heel. no bandaids left. no gel insoles. no allbirds. just the leather loafers she swore she'd break in on vacation. nine miles a day in them. Warrior behavior and completely wrong

**Base video:** Creator sat on a curb or low wall in a city street, sunglasses pushed up, eating something from a paper bag, glancing down the road and then flatly at the lens.  
**Audio:** slow, slightly sad trending sound (the kind used for quiet defeat)  
**Caption:** who is breaking in shoes on vacation  
**Why it is this brand:** Breaking in leather loafers over a city break is the precise pain Allbirds' walk-all-day comfort replaces, told with blistered heels and dead bandaids.

Score 8/10. Editor: replace 'Warrior behavior and completely wrong' with a fresh verdict, it is a near-copy of the sample line 'built different and wrong'.

# HardLaunch: long overlay

**Format decision:** long_overlay. HardLaunch is a SaaS whose pain is an ops nightmare, chasing creators on WhatsApp, casting calls, invoice spreadsheets, and that whole 'old way' lands harder as a silent deadpan reaction to a block of text than as a product demo. The audience of media buyers and agency operators will recognise themselves in the 11pm CPM-climbing scene instantly, which drives the shares and tagged-colleague comments that awareness posts need. Runner-up hook_demo: It needs real recast before/after footage the brand has to supply, and leading with the face-swap mechanic invites the consent and deepfake comment fights the brand explicitly avoids.

## The post

> watched a growth guy set up fifty posting accounts then realize every single one needs a different person on camera. no swaps. no recasts. no hardlaunch. just him in the mirror doing fifty different voices for fifty different brands. we lost a good one today

**Base video:** Creator stands in a bedroom holding two phones, looking slowly left then right off camera like they are trying to find who said that, then back to lens.  
**Audio:** original audio, room tone  
**Caption:** how many accounts are you running right now  
**Why it is this brand:** Fifty warmed posting accounts all needing a different face on camera is the exact wall this product is built at.

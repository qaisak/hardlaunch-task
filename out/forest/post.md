# Forest: long overlay

**Format decision:** long_overlay. Forest is an app whose whole value is a feeling shift, phone face down for 40 minutes with a tree at the end, and the 'old way' list is unusually rich (screen time limits, deleting Instagram and redownloading it, phone across the room) so a silent reacting face plus one long text block lets the viewer recognise themselves before the product is even named. The tree-dies stake is a text reveal, not something that needs footage, so this format carries the whole story cheaply and repeats across the student, remote worker and dinner table scenes. Runner-up hook_demo: The demo is just a timer counting down and a tree drawing itself, which is quiet on screen and needs brand-supplied footage, so it pays off better later as a conversion post than as a first awareness hit.

## The post

> i keep deleting instagram. no screen time limits. no greyscale. no forest. just me and the app store search bar at midnight typing the same nine letters like a password i can't forget. rock bottom has wifi

**Base video:** Person lying back against a headboard in a dark room, phone light on their face, looking dead into the lens with zero expression for the full clip.  
**Audio:** slow melancholy trending sound, low volume  
**Caption:** how many times have you redownloaded it
**Why it is this brand:** The delete-and-redownload cycle is the screen-time failure loop Forest's Deep Focus blocking is the alternative to.

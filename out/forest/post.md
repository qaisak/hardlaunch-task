# Forest: long overlay

**Format decision:** long_overlay. Forest is an app whose value is a felt internal shift, not a physical object, and its 'old way' (picking up the phone for one second and losing forty minutes to TikTok) is instantly relatable as a silent text confession over a reacting face. The tree-growing mechanic lands better as a late reveal in text than as a screen recording, which keeps the awareness post feeling like a personal admission rather than an app demo. Runner-up hook_demo: A screen-recorded timer is visually static and reads as an ad within the first second, killing hold rate on a first awareness post.

## The post

> my brother put his entire phone on greyscale to stop scrolling. no app blocking. no forest. no screen time limit. just a grey tiktok he watched for fifty minutes anyway. he said it felt less fun and stayed for all fifty minutes. Colour was never the problem.

**Draft visual direction (not the selected clip):** Creator on a sofa, head tilted, eyebrows slightly raised, holding their own phone screen-down on their knee. One serious look at camera, one small tilt the other way.
**Audio suggestion (not included):** original audio, room tone
**Caption:** the feed got duller. the fifty minutes didn't.
**Why it is this brand:** Free app blocking that protects the whole session, rather than dulling one feed, is Forest's Deep Focus.

Draft scored 8/10. Editor fix applied: Merge 'he said it felt less fun. he still finished the fifty minutes.' into one beat so the verdict 'colour was never the problem' hits without a pause before it.
What changed: Merged the two closing observations into one beat so the verdict lands with no pause before it.

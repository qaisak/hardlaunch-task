# Share the demo

Public demo: https://ships-intent-convicted-anaheim.trycloudflare.com/r/opal

No login is required. Visitors can play examples, download videos, edit, rebuild and generate from a brand URL.

This is a temporary Cloudflare Quick Tunnel to this computer. Keep the computer awake, connected to the internet, and both app.py and cloudflared running. Restarting the tunnel creates a different URL. Closing the browser does not stop the server.

The tunnel process is recorded in .tools/tunnel.log. To stop sharing, stop the cloudflared process for this project. Local work remains available at http://localhost:8000.
